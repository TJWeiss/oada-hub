    How This Budget Works

    You pay each vendor directly, at the rate in the bid you approved, and OADA bills its own fees separately. Nothing in the vendor columns passes through OADA.

    The project management fee is 8.5% of each line item, calculated on whichever is lower, the approved bid or the vendor's final invoice. If a vendor comes in over the bid, OADA's fee stays at the bid amount; if a vendor comes in under, the fee drops with it. Fees for open phases are shown as projected and are billed once the phase closes and final invoices are in.

    Variance compares each vendor's final invoice to the approved bid. It is shown only for line items that have been invoiced.
